`mailto:` can automatically trigger the system's default email client and pre-fill the recipient, CC, BCC, subject, and body content.

**Parameter details:**  
`mailto:`: Recipient(s), separate multiple with `;`  
`cc`: CC recipient(s), separate multiple with `;`  
`bcc`: BCC recipient(s), separate multiple with `;`  
`subject`: Subject line  
`body`: Email body content

Just connect the parameters using `&amp;`.

Example: `&lt;a mailto:\*\*\*@\*\*\*.com?subject=this is subject&amp;body=this is body&gt;…&lt;a&gt;`

Note: In Elementor, you should enter `mailto:\*\*\*@\*\*\*.com?subject=this is subject&amp;body=this is body` directly into the hyperlink option of the text editor.
