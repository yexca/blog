While miHoYo's HoYoLAB provides a way to check Genshin Impact Resin, it's a bit of a pain to have to reach for your phone and open the app every time you're working on your PC.

Original post: **[I made a Resin tracking tool](https://ngabbs.com/read.php?tid=29226061&rand=307)**

Download link: [Extraction code: 1w5b](https://pan.baidu.com/s/1esw3JjgfMZwtzh1nRGOF7Q)

After downloading and opening the app, it will prompt you for your UID and Cookie. Here is how to grab your HoYoLAB cookie.

First, go to [HoYoLAB](https://bbs.mihoyo.com/ys/) and log in.

Press `F12` to open DevTools, then refresh the page.

Go to the "Network" tab, look for the "ys/" request, find the Request Headers, and right-click the cookie to copy it.

![image](https://jsd.cdn.zzko.cn/gh/yexca/picx-images-hosting@master/2021/11-原神/image.5c0pgzw6pa.webp)

Note: The required cookie fields are `account_id=xxx; cookie_token=xxx`. Please format them yourself.

**Updated 2022-06-06 ([hiyoung](https://blog.hiyoung.xyz))**

Since miHoYo updated the BBS cookie handling, a standard grab might result in missing data. Follow these additional steps:

Open DevTools by pressing `F12` or right-clicking and selecting "Inspect," then go to the Console tab.

Enter the following:

```javascript
var cookie=document.cookie;var ask=confirm('Cookie:'+cookie+'\n\nDo you want to copy the cookie to the clipboard?');if(ask==true){copy(cookie);msg=cookie}else{msg='Cancel'}
```

Hit Enter and click OK once you've confirmed. The cookie is now copied to your clipboard.

**Note**: Use Chrome. Capturing cookies via Edge may result in incomplete data ([yexca](https://lit.link/yexca)).
