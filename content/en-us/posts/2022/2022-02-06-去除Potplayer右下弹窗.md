## Intro

Potplayer is an excellent video player. However, recent versions have started showing annoying pop-up ads in the bottom-right corner. This article covers two methods to get rid of them.

## Use an Older Version Without Pop-ups (Recommended)

The last version without ad pop-ups is 1.7.18958.

All old PotPlayer versions can be downloaded from:
[PotPlayer Old Versions Downloads](https://www.videohelp.com/software/PotPlayer/old-versions)

Direct download for version 1.7.18958: [64-bit](https://www.videohelp.com/download/PotPlayerSetup64-1.7.18958.exe) | [32-bit](https://www.videohelp.com/download/PotPlayerSetup-1.7.18958.exe)

After installation, open Settings (F5). Under "Basic - Auto Update", select "Do not use auto update."
