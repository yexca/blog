## Introduction

This article is a collection of free image hosting services I've gathered. I have personally used some of these. If you are looking to set up your own, I recommend reading [Building a Free Image Host with PicX – yexca’Blog](https://blog.yexca.net/archives/27/).

## 1. ImgTU (路过图床)

Official Link: [ImgTU – Free Image Upload, Professional Image Hosting](https://imgtu.com/)

Fast and stable. Maximum file size is 10MB per image.

## 2. ImgURL

Official Link: [ImgURL Free Image Hosting](https://imgurl.org/)

Guest users are limited to 10 uploads per day. Maximum file size is 5MB per image.

## 3. SM.MS

Official Link: [Image Upload – SM.MS – Simple Free Image Hosting](https://sm.ms/)

A veteran service. Guest uploads are now disabled. Free accounts get 5GB of storage and a 5MB limit per image.

(Note: The direct link domain for this service is loli.net).

## 4. KinhPicture

Official Link: [KinhPicture – Aggregated Image Hosting](https://img.kinh.cc/)

An aggregated image host supporting 15 interfaces including Alibaba, Baidu, and AWS S3. Image limits range from 2MB to 32MB.

## 5. Jitu (极兔图床)

Official Link: [Jitu – Free Image Hosting, Anonymous Hosting, CDN Hosting, Image API](https://pic.jitudisk.com/)

Supports uploads up to 10MB per image.

## 6. Gimhoy

Official Link: [Gimhoy – Unlimited Permanent Free Image Hosting](https://pic.gimhoy.com/)

## 7. bayimg

Official Link: [bayimg – free uncensored image hosting](https://bayimg.com/)
