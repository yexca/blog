## 1. Get the Timestamp

Open this page: <https://member.bilibili.com/x2/creative/h5/calendar/event?ts=0>  
Press `CTRL+F`, type `jointime`, and the number after `jointime` is your timestamp.

![img](https://cdn.statically.io/gh/yexca/picx-images-hosting@master/2022/04-B站注册时间/jointime.2nnsmeqoq2a0.webp)

Copy the timestamp.

## 2. Convert the Timestamp

Go to this website: [Timestamp (Unix timestamp) Converter – Online Tool](https://tool.lu/timestamp/)

Enter the timestamp in the designated field and click convert.

![img](https://cdn.statically.io/gh/yexca/picx-images-hosting@master/2022/04-B站注册时间/时间戳转换.w9edotfdjhc.webp)
