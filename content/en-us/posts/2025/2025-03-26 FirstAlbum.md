## 「まだ雨はやまない」

On 2025-03-23, I grabbed my first physical album: Shigure Ui's 'まだ雨はやまない'. The album art, like a photo album, is just too cute!

![shigureui](https://github.com/yexca/picx-images-hosting/raw/master/2025/03-FirstAlbum/shigureui.6wqw9tfyyd.webp)

But the purchase journey itself was a bit convoluted. I've bought too many things that looked cool at first glance but ended up being disappointing. Plus, budget constraints mean I always have to weigh decisions when buying non-essentials. So, getting an album from an artist I barely knew was definitely a risky move (it wasn't cheap, doge).

I first spotted this album in a physical store back in January (2025). Thought, 'that's adorable,' and the seed to buy it was planted. I also learned about Shigure Ui. Then, on March 22nd, at an exhibition in Okinawa held on VRChat, I saw that Shigure Ui designed Hatsune Miku's image for the event. Felt instantly familiar (and caught the March 23rd livestream).

That got me thinking about the album again. That day, I looked her up, listened to a few tracks, and thought they were pretty good. But when I went to buy it on the 23rd, and actually got to the store, I hesitated. Once I had the album in hand, I started checking it out (I was super torn; usually if I'm buying something, I don't research it beforehand – I want the surprise. If I research, I usually don't buy). But I guess I still had that buying urge. I just looked at the tracklist, and since only one song seemed familiar, I popped into the manga store next door, grabbed a comic, and headed home. On the subway, I reflexively opened my music app. Suddenly, a song hit differently – it was amazing! I instinctively hit 'favorite,' but then saw the title and realized, 'Wait, that's from *that* album!' I immediately U-turned, rushed back to the store, and snagged the album. (Rainy Lady is just *that* good!)

## 「アルストロメリア」

Yesterday (25th), my plans to game with a friend got pushed because they wanted to go out. So, I figured, why not go out too? And just like that, I stumbled upon Kano's album!!!

![kano](https://github.com/yexca/picx-images-hosting/raw/master/2025/03-FirstAlbum/kano.9kgck68skl.webp)

I grabbed it on the spot! My excitement was off the charts. My meager writing skills can't even begin to express how thrilled I was. I'm super familiar with Kano, so no ripping needed for these tracks. Opening the album, heart pounding, and seeing that final smile? My soul was completely healed. What an awesome experience. Seriously, buying stuff you love is always a good move!

![kano-simle](https://github.com/yexca/picx-images-hosting/raw/master/2025/03-FirstAlbum/kano-smile.6t7ac3xf84.webp)

In this moment, only one phrase came to mind: 'このような幸せ、あるでしょうか' (Could there be any happiness like this in the world?)
