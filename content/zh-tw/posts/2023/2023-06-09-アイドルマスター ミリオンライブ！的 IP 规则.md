## clash

透過設定群組來進行更好的選擇

首先設定 JP 群組

```yaml
proxy-groups:
- name: JP
  type: select
  proxies:
  - your jp proxy name
  - your jp proxy name
```

然後新增規則

```yaml
rules:
  - DOMAIN-SUFFIX,theaterdays.appspot.com,JP
```
